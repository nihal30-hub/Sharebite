How ShareBite Works

A seamless three-step platform ensuring good food never goes to waste.

01

Donors Post Surplus

Donors quickly list edible excess food specifying item details, quantity, pickup location, preparation time, and safe pickup deadline.

02

Receivers Browse & Request

Individuals, shelters, and food volunteers filter available listings in their locality and request the precise quantity needed.

03

Accept & Collect

Donors accept requests, quantities are updated automatically, and receivers collect fresh food before the safe pickup deadline.

Key Features

Built for Efficiency and Safety

Engineered with modern cloud architecture to deliver rapid, secure, and reliable food redistribution.

Real-Time Inventory

Available quantities update atomically in Firestore when requests are accepted, preventing duplicate claims or over-allocation.

Safe Pickup Deadlines

Every listing requires a safe consumption deadline to ensure recipients receive fresh, hygienic, and wholesome food.

Role-Based Access

Strict Firebase Security Rules isolate donor listing management from receiver requesting permissions.

Live Impact Analytics

Track real-time community impact: meals rescued, verified handoffs completed, and total kilograms of food saved.

Instant Smart Filters

Filter surplus meals by category—Cooked, Bakery, Produce, Dairy, Packaged—and instant keyword search.

Mobile Responsive

Optimized for on-the-go smartphones, tablets, and laptops so donors and receivers can connect anywhere.

Real Food, Real Impact

Every single portion registered on ShareBite represents carbon emissions prevented and a hungry person nourished. All statistics reflect live Firestore database records.
